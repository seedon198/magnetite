# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone. We
expect participants to be welcoming, patient and respectful of differing
viewpoints and experience levels, to give and gracefully accept constructive
feedback, and to focus on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and any
conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and security ethics

Because this project builds offensive-security capabilities, participants are
also expected to uphold its ethical boundary: tooling and discussion here are
for **authorized** testing, research and education. Using the project, its
issue tracker or its community spaces to plan, solicit or celebrate
unauthorized intrusion or harm is a conduct violation. See the
[threat model](docs/architecture/threat-model.md).

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer via
[GitHub](https://github.com/seedon198). Reports are handled promptly and
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may
ban contributors who violate this code, following the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
